# Voax | The DMing app for you!

Welcome to Voax! We are a fast, lightweight, and secure chat platform built for gamers. 

Voax was created by the power team of Shahadah Studios Elite and BloxdLabs. We built this platform from the ground up to be a clean, zero-lag alternative to standard chat apps.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Easy Login**: Sign up with your email and username, or log in instantly using your Discord account. (We will soon have google! XD)
* **Stable PWA**: Download Voax straight to your desktop or mobile home screen. It works perfectly on low-spec devices, including Chromebooks, with zero lag. (Throw an issue If there is a bug or 2)
* **Smart Chat History**: Chat history loads quickly in small blocks of messages. This keeps the app fast and saves your internet data!
* **Secure Voice Calls**: Call your close friends directly in your Direct Messages (DMs).
* **Server Invites**: Share your server easily with your friends using direct invite links.
